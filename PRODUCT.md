# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A broad professional audience spanning senior engineering leaders and hiring decision-makers, design and engineering peers, and potential collaborators. Visitors need to understand Filip Mares's role, scope, technical leadership, product judgment, and career progression quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present a credible, memorable portfolio of senior product-engineering work. Success means visitors can identify Filip's current focus, understand his specific contribution and impact on major products, inspect representative projects, and leave with confidence in both his engineering depth and design sensibility.

## Positioning

A personal engineering portfolio organized around product work, individual contribution, and career progression rather than a generic list of technologies. Project context connects engineering depth with product and interaction-design judgment.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a public website on desktop or mobile, scan the homepage's project and experience indexes, and open individual case studies or the about page. Project context is available through pointer and keyboard previews on wide screens and inline descriptions on narrower screens. About and LinkedIn links provide further professional context.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Astro website deployed to GitHub Pages from `main`, with `https://filipmares.com` configured as the canonical site.
- Homepage, about page, four project case studies, custom 404, RSS feed, sitemap, and generated social-sharing images.
- Light and dark themes, keyboard focus feedback, reduced-motion support, optional mechanical theme-toggle sound, cycling San Francisco icons, and a hidden blueprint mode.
- Preserve factual project names, dates, roles, and contribution claims. Do not invent outcomes, metrics, testimonials, or project imagery.
- No formal accessibility-conformance claim or quantitative conversion target is established.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Braun and Dieter Rams-inspired identity through usefulness, clarity, restraint, and careful interaction design, rather than nostalgic imitation. Existing voice, assets, and personal signatures remain part of the portfolio.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, and quietly confident. The experience should translate Braun and Dieter Rams-era principles into a contemporary digital system through usefulness, legibility, hierarchy, consistency, restraint, and attention to detail.

## Anti-references

Superficial retro-Braun imitation, ornamental industrial-design motifs, generic portfolio templates, nostalgic skeuomorphism, decorative technical diagrams, and any treatment that makes the work harder to understand. The site should not confuse aesthetic cosplay with functional clarity.

## Evidence on Hand

- `src/content/projects/`: authored case studies for Copilot, Power Apps, Lobe, and Skype.
- `src/pages/index.astro` and `src/pages/about.astro`: current introduction, experience chronology, and biography.
- `public/profile.jpg`, `public/favicon.svg`, and `public/favicon.ico`: existing portrait and identity assets.
- `src/pages/og/`: generated sharing-image routes; `_original-html/` contains historical source references, not the current visual authority.
- Do not treat descriptions or diagrams as independently verified performance evidence.

## Product Principles

1. Make the work and individual contribution unmistakable.
2. Let structure, proportion, and typography carry the identity.
3. Use every decorative or interactive detail to improve meaning or orientation.
4. Preserve disciplined consistency while giving each project enough specificity to feel real.
5. Prefer honest evidence and direct language over abstract claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Pursue practical accessibility improvements without claiming a formal conformance target. Prioritize legible contrast, semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, generous touch targets, reduced-motion support, clear interaction states, responsive layouts, and usable behavior at browser zoom.
