Led UX engineering across Power Apps' AI, Copilot, and collaboration efforts.

Most recently I led UX engineering for Power Apps Agent Builder from inception through its public preview at Microsoft Ignite 2024 and on to product-market fit — growing it into the largest source of Copilot agents created in Microsoft Copilot Studio. Earlier, I managed the team building App Copilot and multiplayer collaboration; the App Copilot platform went on to power Dynamics 365 Copilot for Sales, serving over 500K monthly active users as the fastest-growing feature in Power Apps.

Before that, I led teams shipping Cards for Power Apps (public preview at the Microsoft Power Platform Conference) and AI Builder, where we delivered its first Bring Your Own Model integration for enterprise customers.

## Focus

Architecting robust, low-latency collaboration and human-in-the-loop agent experiences — centralized error handling, optimistic updates, and smooth transitions that make AI-assisted app building feel delightful and reliable.
